# Bumply — AI Pregnancy Companion

Bumply is an AI-powered pregnancy companion designed to provide personalized, accessible, and culturally relevant support for expectant mothers.

Bumply is a technology product developed under **The Brand Nerve**, a Nigerian creative technology venture focused on building innovative digital products and AI-powered solutions.

## 🌍 The Problem

Pregnancy can be overwhelming, especially for first-time mothers who may have questions about symptoms, nutrition, pregnancy milestones, appointments, and everyday decisions.

Many existing pregnancy platforms are designed primarily around Western healthcare contexts and may not adequately reflect the realities, culture, language, food, and everyday experiences of African mothers.

Bumply aims to bridge this gap by providing an AI-powered pregnancy companion designed with African users in mind.

## 💡 The Solution

Bumply provides expectant mothers with an accessible digital companion that can help them:

- Understand pregnancy milestones
- Receive personalized pregnancy information
- Learn about nutrition and pregnancy-related topics
- Ask pregnancy-related questions
- Receive culturally relevant guidance
- Track important pregnancy information
- Access AI-powered conversational support

Bumply is designed to complement professional medical care and does not replace qualified healthcare professionals.

## 🚀 Product Status

Bumply currently exists as a working product/prototype with an active web deployment and ongoing development.

The project is being developed toward a scalable platform that can provide AI-powered pregnancy support to mothers across Nigeria and eventually other African markets.
